# Product Strategy & OKRs: Fable Growth

> Module 1 · Write a Strategy That Can Survive a Test

## 1. Playing to Win cascade

| Question | Your choice |
|---|---|
| **Winning aspiration** | Winning is the app people keep using after the crisis ends, because it gives them a clear, personalized path from feeling better to living better. Fable becomes the place where they build the skills, knowledge and habits that make them more resilient, through digital pathways and small peer cohorts that keep them accountable. |
| **Where to play** | We focus on adults and young adults who have come through a high-stress period and want to keep improving their well-being, staying in the same markets for the first sprint, and considering an expansion strategy in parallel. Our use case is holistic well-being growth for people who are no longer in acute distress, and we leave out clinical treatment, diagnosis and crisis care. |
| **How to win** | We already hold a large base of users who came to us in their hardest moments, and we have the drop-off data to show exactly when and why they leave. With our clinical advisors and content team, we can guide each person from recovery into a curated growth pathway that competitors cannot match. |
| **Capabilities required** | We must be excellent at recovery-stage personalization, at building growth pathways grounded in how people get better after acute distress, and at clinical oversight that provides effective, personalised, get-better interventions such as Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Person-Centred Therapy (PCT), Somatic Therapy – matching effectively customers to the right approach based on their previous data and insights. |
| **Management systems** | We track retention by cohort at 10, 15, 30, 60 and 120 days and review performance every week. This lets us see early whether users are moving onto pathways and staying past the point where they used to leave and which interventions are the most effective. |

## 2. Your one hard no

> **Hard no:** We will not build 1:1 coaching or live, staff-led learning (online or offline), because we do not have the people to deliver it, and it would not scale across millions of users.

## 3. OKR cascade

> **Objective:** Become the personalized companion that keeps improving people’s mental well-being long after the crisis has passed.
>
> - **KR1:** The share of users who are still active in the 90 days after day 90 doubles.
> - **KR2:** The share of users who start a growth pathway within 30 days of finishing their acute phase rises from 0% to 35%.
> - **KR3:** Conversion from free to premium among deeply engaged users doubles.

## 4. AI pressure-test

**Most valid challenge:** Pick one thing Fable becomes after the acute phase. The “hard no” to reconsider: community; The things that more urgently need a hard no are: 1:1 coaching and and live learning

**What I will change and what I will defend:** Updated hard no as it did not fit team size and capabilities; updated objective to really focus on the one thing it would do.
