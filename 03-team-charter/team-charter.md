# Team Charter: Fable Growth

> Module 3 · Set the Habits That Make a Team Faster

## 1. What We Own

> **We own:** PM 1 owns onboarding, PM 2 owns the post-acute journey (pathways, the daily routine and welcoming people back), and PM 3 owns the AI check-in layer. The Core product lead owns the home screen, the one-prompt-a-day limit, using Fable across devices and the progress record.
>
> **Out of scope:** Growth owns acquisition, pricing, the paywall, outreach to lapsed users and peer groups. Clinical advisory owns safety sign-off for content and AI responses, and Legal owns the regulatory review for therapy skills and the therapy handover.
>
> **Joint calls:** hese need a joint call before anyone acts: changing when the acute phase ends (PM 1 and PM 2), adding an AI check-in to a flow (PM 3 and the PM who owns that flow), adding an upsell inside onboarding or retention (Growth and the PM who owns that flow), and any prompt that breaks the one-a-day limit (the Core product lead and the PM asking for it).

## 2. How We Decide

> **Who decides scope:** Each PM decides scope in their own area, and the Core product lead decides any change to the scope or timing of this quarter's roadmap. Clinical advisory can block a release on safety grounds.
>
> **How cross-team conflicts escalate:** If the PMs haven't agreed within two working days, the PM who raised it posts a one-page note with the options in the product channel and tags the Core product lead, who makes the final call within three working days. If Growth is involved, the note goes to the Head of Product instead, who makes the final call within five working days.
